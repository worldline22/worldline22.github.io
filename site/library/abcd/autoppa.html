01

First, picture the problem.

A feedback loop can rediscover the same transformation many times. Handwritten rule libraries take expert effort and cover only selected patterns.

↳After improving a recipe, record what changed and when that change helps—not just the final dish.
02

Make it concrete.

When can two operations share a unit?

Interactive · toy example

A toy learned rule says to share an adder when two uses never overlap. Change the schedule to see why the condition matters.

Two additions in separate time slots may share one adder. Two additions required in the same slot cannot use that same one-adder schedule without delaying work. The rule must include its applicability condition.

Invented teaching example. Values and costs here are not measurements from the paper.

03

What did the paper add?

01

Explore and evaluate

Generate contrasting RTL pairs and assess equivalence and PPA.

02

Induce and validate

Extract conditions and actions, then test whether applying a rule recreates useful changes.

03

Search adaptively

Retrieve suitable rules and combine them through multi-step optimization.

The contribution: Automatic rule-library learning coupled to adaptive rule-based RTL optimization.

04

Bring the idea to ABCD.

A possible research direction
Measured implementation pairs→Conditional rewrite rules→Reusable optimization knowledge

If ABCD retains optimization experience, compare a reusable rule mechanism with AutoPPA’s learned conditions and actions.

Small experiment: Learn a rule on one circuit and apply it to held-out variants, including a deliberately invalid case. Report correctness and cost regressions.

Keep in mind: A learned rule can be wrong or context-specific. Recheck transformed circuits and measure physical costs each time.

This connection is an interpretation for the project, not an implementation or performance claim by the paper.

05

Read the result in context.

Evaluation suite
60 designs

The paper evaluates a collected circuit suite using synthesized PPA metrics.

Default evaluation technology
45 nm flow

Experiments use SiliconCompiler and FreePDK 45 nm unless otherwise specified.

Scope matters. Baseline coverage differs across comparisons. Testbench coverage and repeated successful rewrites do not by themselves establish formal equivalence for all possible inputs.
