01

First, picture the problem.

Correctness checks and physical metrics have different costs and reward scales. Combining them carelessly can encourage invalid or poorly balanced designs.

↳A race entry must pass inspection before its finishing time can earn a prize.
02

Make it concrete.

Which checks can this candidate reach?

Interactive · toy example

Try a syntax error, a wrong answer, or a correct toy circuit. Follow the gates before the PPA score.

Syntax error stops at compilation. Compiling code with the wrong output stops at the functional check. A candidate passing both can proceed to synthesis and PPA scoring. Passing a finite test suite is still not a formal proof.

Invented teaching example. Values and costs here are not measurements from the paper.

03

What did the paper add?

01

Stage the rewards

Use progressively more demanding tool checks with gates before downstream evaluation.

02

Adapt training priorities

A curriculum changes the importance of easier process rewards versus the final objectives.

03

Express preferences

Prompt-conditioned PPA weights let training address different cost priorities.

The contribution: EDA-integrated reinforcement learning with staged rewards and curriculum-guided policy optimization for Verilog generation.

04

Bring the idea to ABCD.

A possible research direction
Generated RTL→Tool-checked training feedback→Correctness + PPA preferences

If ABCD trains models, separate invalid outputs from physically poor but correct outputs in the reward design.

Small experiment: Count tool invocations and retained candidates with and without early validity gates, using the same candidate set.

Keep in mind: This training hierarchy is not evidence of hierarchical hardware composition. Reward quality depends on tests and the implementation flow.

This connection is an interpretation for the project, not an implementation or performance claim by the paper.

05

Read the result in context.

Model backbones
4

The paper reports results for multiple code-model backbones, rather than one universal generator.

Correctness benchmarks
2

VerilogEval and RTLLM v1.1 are evaluated alongside PPA-oriented results.

Scope matters. Correctness rates and PPA improvements use particular models, test suites, and tool settings. They do not establish that every generated circuit is correct or budget-compliant.
