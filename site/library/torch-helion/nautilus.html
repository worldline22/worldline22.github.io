01

First, picture the problem.

A mathematical formula does not say which intermediate arrays to store, which loops to fuse, or what tile sizes fit the GPU. Those choices can dominate execution time.

↳Instead of keeping every receipt and totaling them at the end, keep a running total and update it whenever another receipt arrives.
02

Make it concrete.

A weighted average without keeping every item

Interactive · toy example

Item 1 has value 10 and weight 1. Item 2 has value 20 and weight 2. Update a numerator and denominator instead of storing the whole list.

First item: numerator = 1 × 10 = 10, denominator = 1, average = 10. Add item 2: numerator = 10 + 2 × 20 = 50; denominator = 1 + 2 = 3; average = 50 / 3 ≈ 16.67. Averaging the two values directly would incorrectly give 15.

Invented teaching example. Values and costs here are not measurements from the paper.

03

What did the paper add?

01

Generate schedules

Producer–consumer passes choose two levels of tiling, fusion, localization, and regular inner loops.

02

Rewrite in stages

Intermediate representations expose algebraic simplifications and tile optimizations. Rolling-update fusion builds on Neptune.

03

Tune and lower

Profile schedule parameters and emit code through GPU tile backends such as CuTe and TileLang.

The contribution: A coordinated auto-scheduling and lowering pipeline that discovers efficient tiled GPU implementations from tensor expressions, including attention variants.

04

Bring the idea to Torch-Helion.

A possible research direction
Tensor expression→Fusion + tiling + rewriting→Tuned tile kernel

Explore schedule transformations above Helion’s tile code, then use the backend to tune the resulting implementations. Treat fusion structure and tile parameters as related decisions.

Small experiment: For a small normalized weighted sum, compare materializing all weights with a running-state version. Measure intermediate storage, arithmetic, latency, and numerical error.

Keep in mind: The toy average only explains running state. Real attention needs stable softmax rescaling and a valid transformation of all dependent computations.

This connection is an interpretation for the project, not an implementation or performance claim by the paper.
